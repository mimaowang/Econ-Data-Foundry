# Maintainers

The repository owner and designated reviewers maintain the catalog, schemas, automation, generated artifacts, and public documentation.

Maintainer responsibilities include:

- protecting the core idea-to-dataset matching standard;
- reviewing evidence, access routes, licensing notes, and status changes;
- keeping validation, exports, tests, and generated views reproducible;
- responding to security, privacy, and provider-terms concerns;
- avoiding irreversible automation and preserving a clear ledger history.

People with repository write access are not automatically authorized to promote uncertain records to `ready`. That decision should remain evidence-based and reviewable.
